**Deploy: v0.314.0 — the customer portal's deliveries and lots (FSMA 204 for a first receiver)**

**No migrate.** Tools stay at 1,148.

**What changed** (docs/design/customer_portal_plan.md)
- **Deliveries** (new section, on by default): the scale tickets delivered to THIS customer — by the ticket's
  customer, or by its Settlement Statement being the customer's. Shown: date, ticket, block ticker, variety, grade,
  net weight and unit, destination, and the lot. Never a price.
- **Lots** (new section, on by default): the Traceability Lot Codes the customer received — the lots its tickets
  resolve to (block, variety, day), plus any lot a Shipping CTE names it as receiver of.
  - Each lot carries its FSMA 204 critical tracking events with the KDEs: event, date and time, location (block
    ticker), quantity and unit, ship-from, ship-to, receiver, carrier and reference type.
  - A Shipping CTE to another customer is left out, and no person (actor, description) is ever shown.
- **Downloads:**
  - `format=fsma204`: the FSMA 204 sortable spreadsheet, one row per critical tracking event — the 24-hour request
    format.
  - The XLSX pack gains Deliveries and FSMA 204 sheets; the PDF pack gains Deliveries and Lots received.
  - The JSON feed carries both new sections.
- **Toggles:** both sections are toggled per portal (`sections: {deliveries: 0/1, lots: 0/1}`).

**Build / server**

Push and build with `ERPNEXT_MCP_VERSION=0.314.0`, then `bench --site frontend migrate` (nothing new) and restart.

**Rollback**

Roll back to the v0.313.0 image. The two sections disappear.
