**Deploy: v0.313.0 — the customer / first-handler portal (was the packer portal)**

**Migrate**:
- `Packer Share` → `Customer Portal` and `Packer Share Block` → `Customer Portal Block`. A pre-sync patch renames
  them where an older version installed them; both of Tim's servers are on v0.269.0, so they get the new names
  directly.
- Customer Portal: `customer` is now required, and `customer_role` (Packer / Buyer / First Handler) is new.
- Share Link `kind` gains "Customer Portal".
- Six new `allow_` switches. A post-sync patch turns each one on where its packer-named switch was on.

Tools: **1,142 → 1,148** (six generic names; the six packer names stay).

**What changed** (Tim, 2026-10-10: a generic customer / first-handler portal; docs/design/customer_portal_plan.md)
- **Who:** any ERPNext Customer that buys, packs or first-handles the fruit — Orchard View, Monson, PFI, a future
  buyer — gets a portal issued by the grower company. Orchard View is just the first configured instance.
- **What they see:** only the blocks committed to that customer, by ticker; sections toggled per portal (sprays with
  EPA number, rate, REI / PHI and MRL by market; IPM; projections; clear to harvest; the feed).
- **Access:** named, revocable credentials; every view and download logged; the public routes rate limited per
  address. Never costs, prices, labour, people or HR.
- **Tools:** `create_customer_portal`, `issue_portal_credential`, `revoke_portal_access`, `get_customer_portal`,
  `get_customer_portal_pack`, `list_portal_access_log`. The packer names stay callable and are logged as superseded.
  `share_link kind=customer_portal`; `packer` still works.
- **Routes:** `/farmops/api/portal/<token>` and `/portal-feed`. The `/packer/…` paths and links issued as "Packer"
  keep working.
- **Switch:** `customer_portal_enabled` per company; the older `packer_portal_enabled` still turns it on.
