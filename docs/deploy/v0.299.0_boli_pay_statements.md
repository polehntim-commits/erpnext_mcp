**Deploy: v0.299.0 — pay statements and the written pay explanation, per Oregon law**

**Migrate.** Farm Payroll Entry gains `pay_date`. Tools stay at 1,142. Existing tools gain arguments:
- `run_payroll_for_period`: `pay_date`;
- `render_pay_stub`: `pay_date`, `company_phone`, and `explanation`.

Only stubs for runs saved after this release carry the full itemization. Older runs keep the balancing line.

**What changed**

ORS 652.610(1)(b) and OAR 839-020-0012 list what every pay statement must show. Each stub now prints:
- **Header:**
  - the date of payment beside the dates of work;
  - the employer's legal name, address and **phone**, and the employer ID (FEIN);
  - how the worker is paid (by the piece or by the hour).
- **Piece work:** each piece rate on its own line, with the pieces completed at it and the pay for it.
- **Hourly work** at its rate.
- **Paid rest periods.**
- **Overtime:** the hours, the overtime rate, and the overtime pay. For piece work, that's half the week's
  regular rate, and the stub states the rate.
- **Bonuses, commissions and profit shares:** each on its own line.
- **Deductions:** each one with its **purpose** (for example "court-ordered child support, case …" or "union
  dues").

ORS 652.610(5) requires a written explanation at hire, updated by January 1 each year:
- `render_pay_stub employee=… explanation=1 [payday="every other Friday"]` produces it in English and Spanish.
- It covers the pay period and payday, the worker's own pay-rate types, the overtime rule in force (Oregon 48/40,
  Washington 40), every deduction and benefit contribution, and what each line on a pay statement means.
- It is attached to the Employee record.

  - Give every new hire one at onboarding.
  - Regenerate them each January.
