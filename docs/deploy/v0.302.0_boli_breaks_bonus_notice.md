Tools stay at 1,142.

**What changed**
1. **BOLI's chart, as data** (oregon.gov/boli/workers/Pages/meals-and-breaks.aspx; OAR 839-020-0050):
   - **Rest:** a paid 10-minute rest per 4-hour segment or major part of one.

     | Shift length | Paid rests |
     |---|---|
     | Over 2 hours, to 6 | 1 |
     | Over 6, to 10 | 2 |
     | Over 10, to 14 | 3 |
     | Over 14, to 18 | 4 |
     | Over 18, to 22 | 5 |
     | Over 22, to 24 | 6 |

   - **Meal:** an unpaid 30-minute meal from 6 hours, a second at 14, a third at 22.
   - `get_break_policy` points at the draft when no Oregon policy is enabled.
2. **Corrected, minors** (OAR 839-021-0072): BOLI says rest breaks for all minors are 15 minutes rather than 10,
   at the adult frequency, with the meal at six hours.
   - The app had a minor's rest every two hours and a meal at four. That's fixed on the server (`minors.py`) and on
     the phone (FarmOps 0.61.0, the offline schedule).
   - Washington minors (WAC 296-125-0287, every two hours) are unchanged.
3. **Harvest-bonus notices** (OAR 839-020-0013): a grower offering a harvest bonus must post its terms, including
   when it's earned, where the crew can read them and in their language. Undisclosed conditions can't be used to
   deny or reduce the bonus. Record the posting on the pay item (`terms`, `terms_posted_on`, `terms_language`);
   each run lists bonuses with no posting on record (`bonus_terms_not_posted`).
