**Deploy: v0.296.0 — non-discretionary bonuses in the overtime regular rate**

**Migrate.** Farm Pay Item gains `earned_from` and `earned_to`, and the kind "Production Bonus". The migrate seeds
payroll setting `pay_item_policy`: every kind is non-discretionary (Bonus, Production Bonus, Profit Share,
Commission, Other). Tools stay at 1,142; `config` takes kind `pay_item_policy`.

**What it does** (Tim, 2026-10-09; FLSA 29 CFR 778.208–.210, applied the same under Oregon's agricultural overtime)

- **The rule:** a non-discretionary bonus raises the regular rate of the workweeks it was earned in. This
  includes the crab-boat profit share and production bonuses.
- **Allocation:** the bonus is spread hour by hour over those weeks (778.209(b)): bonus ÷ hours worked in the
  earning window is the increase per hour. Every overtime hour in those weeks is owed the extra (multiplier − 1)
  of that increase.
- **Payment:** it's paid with the bonus, retroactively, as a wages line "Overtime on bonus (regular rate)" next to
  it, with the week-by-week working on the run.
- **Overtime threshold:** the one in force through the Pay Policy, never below the state's agricultural
  threshold.
- **Earning window:** `earned_from` and `earned_to` on the pay item, snapped to whole workweeks. Blank means the
  pay period it's paid in.
- **Discretionary or not** is a flag per kind in `pay_item_policy`. Marking a kind discretionary leaves it out, and
  the validator warns, because that holds only when the fact and the amount are both at the employer's sole
  discretion (778.211).

**3. Using it**

When entering a bonus or profit share (Farm Pay Item), set **Earned From / Earned To** to the work it was for. Then
`preview_payroll_for_period` shows the derived overtime line and its weekly working before the run is saved.
