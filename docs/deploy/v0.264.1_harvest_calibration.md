**Deploy: v0.264.1 — Tim's harvest windows as calibration proposals**

No migrate is needed. It goes out with the next image, after v0.264.0.

**What it adds**

- **Tim's harvest dates.** Harvest_Dates.numbers is now in the app as seed data: 24 Mill Creek windows and 6 for
  40 Acre, covering 2023, 2025 and 2026.
  - The production tonnage file was not added. It isn't needed for this, and it's business data.
- **A new tool, `propose_harvest_calibration`.** It's a write switch and starts OFF. Given which block each parcel
  is, it proposes two things:
  - **One crop model per variety:** the degree days this farm's own weather had summed to by the first picking
    day, averaged over the seasons. 2023 counts for less, because picking that year followed the market more than
    ripeness.
  - **Parcel offsets:** how much warmer or cooler Mill Creek runs than 40 Acre, centred on zero. These are
    relative only, because the dates are used both to build the models and to compare the parcels.
- **Nothing is applied.** With `draft: true` it writes Draft versions, which a person compares with
  `preview_config` and publishes in the Desk.

**Use it (Tim)**

1. Switch on `allow_propose_harvest_calibration`.
2. Run `propose_harvest_calibration` with `block_map {"Mill Creek": "<Field>", "40 Acre": "<Field>"}`, using this
   site's Field names.
3. Read the proposed models and offsets.
4. Run it again with `draft: true`, then publish the Drafts in the Desk if they hold up.

**Caveats**

- Picking dates follow crews and the market as well as maturity.
- There are only two parcels and three seasons, and no 2024 windows.
- Treat the result as a first estimate to be confirmed by trap catches and stage records.
