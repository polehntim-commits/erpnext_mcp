**What changed in the rules** (DOR Publication 150-211-602, Rev. 02-18-22)
- **Oregon-only exemption:** an exemption claimed for Oregon but not federally is withheld at **8%**, and the
  certificate is flagged to send to the DOR.
- **Expired exemption:** an exemption that expired (February 15) with no new OR-W-4 is withheld at **8%**.
  Before this release both cases used the formula.
- **Not recorded:** an exemption whose federal status isn't recorded keeps its previous treatment and the record
  shows a warning to record it.
- **Line 4a codes** are checked against the 2026 exemption chart (A–H, J, K, L, M).

**For Tim / the CPA**
- **E-signatures:** the DOR has not confirmed it accepts e-signed OR-W-4s. Payroll setting
  `oregon_withholding.esignature.confirmed` stays false until it does, and every OR-W-4 signature and render says
  so. The phone shows a "keep a signed paper copy" banner.
- **Sending copies to the DOR:** "within 20 days" is read from the employee's signature, as a policy value
  (`forwarding.days`). Publication 150-211-602 (2022) no longer lists the send-a-copy triggers; the form itself
  says the employer "may be required to send a copy". They stay as conservative flags sourced to the rule.
