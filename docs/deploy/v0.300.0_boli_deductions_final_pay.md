**Deploy: v0.300.0 — deductions, paydays and final pay, per Oregon law**

**Migrate.**
- Farm Payroll Deduction gains `legal_authority`, `authorized_on`, and the worker's authorization as a signature
  or a scanned form.
- Employee gains `separation_reason`, `seasonal_farmworker` and `final_paid_on` (custom fields).

Tools stay at 1,142. Existing tools gain arguments:
- `create_payroll_deduction` / `update_payroll_deduction`: `legal_authority`, `authorized_on`;
- `update_employee`: `relieving_date`, `separation_reason`, `seasonal_farmworker`, `final_paid_on`;
- `collect_form_signature` takes doctype `Farm Payroll Deduction`, field `authorization_signature`.

**What changed**
1. **ORS 652.610(3), deductions:** a voluntary deduction (401(k), insurance, dues, "other") is taken only with the
   worker's signed written authorization, or a collective bargaining agreement.
   - Court orders, levies and taxes are "required by law" and are unaffected.
   - An election with neither is **not taken**, and the run lists it under `deductions.not_taken_unauthorized`
     with the fix.
2. **ORS 652.120, paydays:** a pay period longer than 35 days is refused.
3. **ORS 652.140 / 652.145, final pay:**
   - Record the last day and the reason with `update_employee`.
   - The payroll calendar then shows the deadline until `final_paid_on` is set:

     | Situation | Final pay due |
     |---|---|
     | Discharged, mutual agreement, or end of season | By the end of the next business day |
     | Quit with 48+ hours' notice | On the last day |
     | Quit without notice | Within 5 business days, or sooner at the next payday |
     | Seasonal farmworker | Immediately (quit without notice: within 48 hours) |

   - Penalty wages run from the deadline (ORS 652.150).

**1. Build (Tim)**

Push erpnext_mcp main (`__version__ = "0.300.0"`), then build with `ERPNEXT_MCP_VERSION=0.300.0`.

**2. Server**

```zsh
sudo docker exec -u frappe -w /home/frappe/frappe-bench fafo-erpnext_server_1 bench --site frontend migrate
sudo docker restart fafo-erpnext_server_1
```

Expect `final-pay fields added: Employee.separation_reason, Employee.seasonal_farmworker, Employee.final_paid_on.`

**3. HR, before the first run with deductions**

`list_payroll_deductions` shows each one's `legal_authority`. For each voluntary deduction, do one of these:
- collect the worker's signature (`collect_form_signature doctype="Farm Payroll Deduction"
  field="authorization_signature"`);
- attach the signed form and set `authorized_on`;
- set `legal_authority` to "Collective bargaining agreement" where a union contract covers it.

Otherwise it isn't taken.

**Rollback**

Roll back to the v0.299.0 image. Elections are then taken without the check, and the calendar loses the final-pay
rows.
