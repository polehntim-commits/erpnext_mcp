**What it does (Tim, 2026-10-06)**

- **A graph Tim can edit.** Crops, pests, diseases, weeds and vertebrates; beneficial insects, mites, microbes and
  vertebrates; pollinators and products. They are linked by attacks, preys on, parasitizes, controls, harmed by,
  hosts, competes with and pollinates. Every link carries:
  - where it came from: Literature, Farm Observed, User Entered, AI Proposed or Imported;
  - a confidence;
  - the crop and BBCH stages it applies to;
  - an on/off switch.
- **Re-seeding is safe.** Only rows still marked Literature are refreshed. A literature link is never edited
  directly: an edit writes the farm's own copy, which then shows in its place.
- **Vertebrates carry their legal status.**
  - Robins, cedar waxwings, house finches, owls, kestrels and hawks are protected by the Migratory Bird Treaty Act
    (MBTA). Crows are protected too, under a federal depredation order.
  - Starlings are not MBTA-protected.
  - Deer are a state game species.
  - For protected birds only non-lethal options are shown: netting, scare devices, raptor perches and nest boxes.
  - A rodenticide is marked as going through the rodent-bait task rules.
- **Lowest impact first.** After a pest is logged, the phone shows where it stands against its threshold, then the
  options. Options that harm nothing working at this stage come first; products are ranked by what they would harm
  among the beneficials and pollinators active now. The label caveat is always shown.
- **Bring in more data.**
  - `import_ipm_graph` takes JSON or CSV. It is a dry run by default and shows what would be added, changed,
    skipped or refused.
  - `export_ipm_graph` writes the same shape back out.
  - `propose_ipm_relationships` is the AI path: an agent reads a handbook page or regulation, cites it, and its
    proposals land OFF for a person to approve (`approve_ipm_proposal`).
  - Nothing caps how big the graph grows; every list pages.
- **On the phone (0.49.0).**
  - Everyone enrolled can open the map and log a pest.
  - Only a Farm Manager, Compliance Officer or System Manager can add or edit a relationship. A Foreman cannot.
  - The map and edits work offline.
