**Deploy: v0.301.0 — Oregon sick time (ORS 653.601–.661, OAR 839-007)**

**Migrate.**
- New doctype **Farm Sick Time Ledger**.
- Farm Pay Item gains kind "Sick Time" and `hours`.
- New payroll setting `sick_time_policy` (seeded).

Tools stay at 1,142. Existing tools gain arguments:
- `create_leave_request`: `leave_type="Oregon Sick Time"` with `hours`;
- `get_leave_balance`: `leave_type="Oregon Sick Time"`, `as_of`;
- `config` takes kind `sick_time_policy`.

**The rules** (`sick_time_policy`, dated and sourced; it can't be set below the law)

| Rule | Setting |
|---|---|
| Accrual | 1 hour per 30 Oregon hours worked, from day one; at most 40 accrued a year |
| Carryover | Up to 40 hours carried into the next year; the balance is capped at 80 |
| Use | Up to 40 hours a year, from the **91st day** of employment |
| Rehire | The balance is restored on rehire within 180 days, including seasonal workers |
| Paid or unpaid | **Paid** when the employer has 10 or more employees (`paid: auto`); otherwise job-protected and unpaid, but still counted. Publish `paid: true` to always pay |
| Pay rate (OAR 839-007-0000(7)) | The regular hourly rate. Piece-only workers get at least minimum wage. Hourly-plus-piece workers get the greater of their hourly rate and minimum wage. Never part of the overtime regular rate |

**How it works**
- **Accrual:** each saved payroll run, daily runs and the settlement included, accrues on Oregon hours. Cancelled
  runs don't count.
- **Filing:** HR, or the worker through HR, files `create_leave_request leave_type="Oregon Sick Time" hours=4
  from_date=…`.
- **Payment:** the run covering that date pays it, within the balance. Anything beyond the balance, or before day
  91, waits with a reason (`pay_items_waiting`).
- **On the stub:** the balance (available, accrued this year, used) and the sick pay line.


Expect `payroll setting payroll_setting:sick_time_policy@1 seeded.`

**Not covered here**
- Accrual starts from this release's runs. Hours worked earlier aren't backfilled. Add an opening balance as a
  ledger row (kind Adjustment) for anyone with prior accrual.
- Washington paid sick leave (RCW 49.46.210, 1 hour per 40) is its own law and isn't covered by this release.
- The phone doesn't file sick time yet.

**Rollback**

Roll back to the v0.300.0 image. The ledger rows stay; Sick Time items are ignored.
