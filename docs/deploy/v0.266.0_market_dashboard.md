**Deploy: v0.266.0 + FarmOps 0.51.0 — the market dashboard, and the market in the pro forma**

Apply after v0.265.0, on umbrel.local first. The migrate adds the `market-prices` page and its shortcut in the
Market & Sales workspace. Deploy the server before FarmOps 0.51.0, because the app needs v0.265.0's routes.

**What it adds**

- **The dashboard.** Desk → Market & Sales → **Market Prices**.
  - **Candles:** one candle per day or week for each cherry size (or cantaloupe count).
    - The body runs from the first to the last quote's "mostly" midpoint; the wick runs from the lowest low to the
      highest high.
    - A volume bar pane sits underneath on the same dates, with a crosshair, zoom and pan.
  - **Gaps:** days with no quote stay empty, and days the report listed without a price show a "no quote" mark.
  - **Overlays:** terminal market (context), grower return $/lb, and breakeven $/lb.
  - **Season over season:** lines aligned by week of the season, plus weekly bar charts by size.
  - **Pro forma ranges:** each season's 10th, 50th and 90th percentile prices.
  - Also on the page: the card and the open data issues.
- **No internet needed.** The chart library is served from the app, not a CDN.
- **Pro forma.** `get_breakeven_sensitivity` now includes what the market actually paid (`market_reference`).
- **Harvest timing.** `get_price_trend` and the dashboard show the week of the season when prices have usually
  peaked, and which week this season is in.

**1. Build (Tim)**

1. Push erpnext_mcp main (`__version__ = "0.266.0"`) and build the image.
2. Archive FarmOps 0.51.0 (46), scheme FarmOps.

**2. Server**

Migrate and restart as usual.

**3. Checks**

1. Open Market Prices with commodity Sweet cherries and size 10 row.
   - Candles and volume line up.
   - Hovering shows open/high/low/close and volume.
   - A day with no quote is an empty slot, not a flat bar.
2. Pick "All sizes": one small chart per size.
3. Tick Terminal, Grower return and Breakeven. The breakeven line sits at $1.22/lb (Constancy 2027 draft) unless a
   Breakeven Analysis in pounds is linked in the Market Commodity config.
4. Season over season and the weekly bars draw once there are two or more seasons (after the backfill).
5. `get_breakeven_sensitivity` on a cherry analysis includes `market_reference`.

**Rollback**

The v0.265.0 image.
