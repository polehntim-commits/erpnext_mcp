**Deploy: v0.308.0 — e-signed W-4 / OR-W-4 to the IRS standard, and Oregon's 8% rule** (pairs with FarmOps 0.63.0 (59))

**Migrate**: Signing Evidence gains `attestation` (read-only). Nothing is rewritten. Tools stay at 1,142.

**What changed**
- **E-signature built to the IRS standard** (26 CFR 31.3402(f)(5)-1(c)) for the federal W-4 box and the OR-W-4 box.
  The policy is `oregon_withholding.esignature`.
  1. **Authenticated signer:** a badge the server resolves, or "Employee ID", an in-person photo-ID check attested
     by a signed-in hiring or HR user. Unverified signatures and a photograph alone are refused.
  2. **Perjury statement:** the form's own statement, shown immediately before the signature, is recorded on the
     Signing Evidence row (`attestation`).
  3. **Final entry:** the signature locks that form's elections. A change means a new W-4 or OR-W-4, signed again.
     The employer's bookkeeping columns stay editable.
  4. **Access log:** every read or render of a signed W-4 is written to Frappe's Access Log.
  5. **Hard copy:** reproducible on the official form (`render_w4_pdf`, `form=or_w4` for the DOR's 150-101-402).
- **DOR status:** "awaiting DOR written confirmation". ORS 84.019 (UETA) generally accepts e-signatures; ORS 84.052
  lets an agency opt out, and the DOR has published neither. Keep a wet-signed copy until the DOR confirms in writing.
- **Correction, OAR 150-316-0234:** with no OR-W-4, Oregon withholding is a flat 8%, not Single with no allowances.
  - This includes a worker with a 2020-or-later federal W-4 on file; the run's detail says so and cites the rule.
  - A pre-2020 federal W-4 used in the fallback order stops counting once a 2020+ federal W-4 is in force.
  - With no forms at all, it's 8%, as the current OR-W-4 instructions say.

**Phones**

FarmOps 0.63.0 (59). With no badge yet, the W-4 sign step asks the hiring user to confirm the in-person ID check,
and the signatures go up as "Employee ID". Phones on 0.62.0 or older get the refusal on an unbadged W-4 signature
until they update.

**Build / server**

Push and build with `ERPNEXT_MCP_VERSION=0.308.0`, then `bench --site frontend migrate` and restart. Install
FarmOps 0.63.0 (59).

**Rollback**

Roll back to the v0.307.0 image. The `attestation` column stays and is ignored, and unverified W-4 signatures are
accepted again.
