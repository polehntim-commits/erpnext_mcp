**What changed — the hotfix (works with FarmOps 0.66.0 as installed)**
- **A Paused job can be completed** (see below).
- **Photo steps met by untagged photos.** Some outstanding checklist steps can be met this way:
  - when every outstanding step has evidence_type Photo, and there are at least that many untagged non-"before"
    photos (on the call or saved earlier), they are matched in the order taken;
  - each step's note names the photo, and the task timeline says "check each shows its side";
  - the completion result lists `photo_matches`.

  This is what 0.66 sends: one before photo, four after photos, no ticks.
- **Late evidence is never refused from a phone.** A completion for an assignment that was since rejected,
  reassigned, held or merged does not complete the job:
  - its photos are added to the worker's own assignment (idempotent) and filed on the asset;
  - the task timeline and Error Log record the mismatch;
  - the phone is answered OK, so its queue drains.
- **Refused phone writes are now visible:** each refused mutating `/mobile` call writes an Error Log row "FarmOps
  sync refused — <method>" with the phone's entry id, at most once an hour per item. MCP Action Log keeps the full
  arguments, as before.
- **Patch `link_orphan_task_photos`:** every unattached File named `FT-…_` (the phone's naming) is attached to its
  task's latest assignment and filed on the asset. This covers Tim's orphaned photos of 2026-10-13. No evidence rows
  are created, so nothing is double-counted. It is idempotent.

**What changed — partial progress (used by FarmOps 0.67.0)**
- **`/mobile/save_task_progress`** saves pieces of an open job (Claimed, In-Progress, Paused or Held). The pieces are:
  - a photo, tagged with the checklist step it proves (`checklist_item`), which ticks that step;
  - a tick;
  - a fix;
  - the findings draft;
  - draft form answers.

  Each piece carries the phone's `client_item_id`. A piece the server has already received answers `duplicate`.
  Each piece is saved or refused on its own, so one bad piece never refuses the batch. The evidence contract is
  not checked here.
- **A photo is attached to the assignment, and also filed on the task's asset**, as a second File row with the same URL.
- **Complete:**
  - **accepts a Paused assignment.** When the server auto-paused the job after it was finished, the clock stops at
    `completed_at`, not at the pause;
  - **merges what was already saved.** Saved photos count toward the contract and are never filed twice. The findings
    draft and the draft answers fill in where the call sent none. A photo tagged with a step ticks it;
  - **on a checklist or evidence refusal, keeps what the call carried** (photos, findings, fix, answers) as progress,
    committed on its own. The response carries `error_detail = {missing: […], kept: {…}, progress: {…}}` beside
    the English sentence.
- **`get_farm_task`:**
  - each open assignment shows `progress` (photos by step, ticks, findings draft, fix);
  - evidence rows carry `checklist_item` and `client_item_id`;
  - phone `get_task` carries `progress` for the caller's open job.
