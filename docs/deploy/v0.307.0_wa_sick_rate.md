**Deploy: v0.307.0 — the Washington paid sick leave rate**

**Migrate**: Farm Pay Item gains `rate_evidence` (read-only JSON). Nothing is rewritten. Tools stay at 1,142.

**What changed**

Washington paid sick leave is paid at the greater of the Washington minimum wage ($17.13 in 2026, from the Wage
Floor) and the worker's normal hourly compensation (WAC 296-128-670; L&I Administrative Policy ES.B.2). This replaces
v0.305.0's interim rate and its WAC 296-128-620 citation, which was the wrong section.
- **One method per job class** (670(6)), in `sick_time_policy.washington.rate_method`:
  - **Piece Rate:** `recent_similar_workweek`. The validator refuses `hourly_rate` for piece work.
  - **Hourly / Salary:** `hourly_rate`.
- **Most recent similar workweek:** the last completed workweek before the leave with Washington work, looking back
  up to `rate_lookback_weeks` (12). The rate is total earnings divided by total hours.
  - **Week choice:** an identical week first (same pay type and kind of shift as the worker's latest), then a
    substantially similar one (same pay type).
  - **Earnings:** piece, hourly, paid rest and downtime, and non-discretionary or production bonuses earned that
    week (pro-rated by the days of their earning window).
  - **Excluded:** the overtime premium, tips, holiday pay, reimbursements and discretionary bonuses.
- **Fallback:** with no similar week, the greater of the most recent agricultural week's rate and the minimum wage.
  With no history at all, the minimum wage. Both are flagged `attorney_review`.
- **Evidence:** the week used and the calculation are written to the pay item's `rate_evidence` when a saved run pays
  it. Oregon items record their rule (OAR 839-007-0000(7)).

**For the attorney**

Any sick pay whose evidence says `attorney_review: true`, which is the fallback cases.

**Build / server**

**Rollback**

Roll back to the v0.306.0 image. The `rate_evidence` column stays and is ignored, and Washington sick pay goes back
