**Deploy: v0.261.0 + FarmOps 0.48.0 (build 43) — claiming a task with no signal**

**Migrate needed**: six columns on Farm Task Assignment, one compliance rule seeded ON, and one write switch
(OFF). **umbrel.local first.** Apply after v0.260.0. Deploy the server before the app: 0.48.0's queued claim
needs v0.261.0 to keep a late claim. Against an older server, a queued claim that lost the race fails in the
queue (Settings → unsent work), as an online claim does today.

**What it does (Tim, 2026-10-06)**

- **Offline claims.** A worker can claim a task from the saved pool with no signal. The claim waits on the phone,
  and the task screen says "Claimed on phone — will confirm when synced". When signal returns, the claim is sent
  ahead of that task's start and completion.
- **First to reach the farm wins.** When two people claimed the same task offline, the first claim to reach the
  server holds the task. The phone clocks don't decide it.
- **Nothing is thrown away.** The later claim becomes a **second claim**. It is its own assignment on the same
  task, with the second worker's start, pauses, completion, minutes, notes and photos kept. Their minutes count
  wherever assignment minutes count, including the cost split payroll reads. The task itself stays with the
  holder: its state, evidence contract, compliance record, stock drawdown and spray windows are not moved by the
  second claim.
- **Flagged for review.** Both assignments carry "Claimed offline by two people". Rule
  `task_claimed_offline_twice` (seeded ON) raises a compliance alert for the Foreman, Farm Manager and HR. A
  supervisor reviews it with `review_claim_conflict` (switch OFF) or by ticking **Conflict Reviewed** on the
  assignment in the Desk, and that clears the alert.
- **The phone tells the second worker.** It shows "Someone else's claim reached the farm first … your time and
  photos are kept", naming who holds the task.
- **A refused claim is still recorded.** An offline claim that a rule would refuse (a Draft task, a crew task, a
  qualification or PHI refusal on an unheld task) is also kept as a second claim, with the reason. The task is
  left as it was.
- **Online claims are unchanged.** A claim made with signal on a task someone holds is still refused.

3. Bring phone A online. The task is A's.
4. Bring phone B online. Phone B shows "Someone else's claim reached the farm first", naming A, and its own start

5. Desk → the task's assignments: two rows. B's has **Second Claim** ticked, and both carry Claim Conflict. The
