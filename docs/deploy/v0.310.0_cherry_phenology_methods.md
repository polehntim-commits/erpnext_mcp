**Deploy: v0.310.0 — cherry phenology methods per variety and block**

**No migrate.** A site with `crop_phenology@1` keeps its published row until a person publishes a new version. The
shipped seed, and any site with nothing published, uses the new default. Tools stay at 1,142.

**What changed** (Tim, 2026-10-10; this supersedes the earlier 40 °F phenology default)
- **Default method `mar1_2c`:** sweet cherry counts from March 1 at 2 °C (35.6 °F) — Kaiser, Coop & Meland, Oregon
  State University 2014. The 'Van' full-bloom stage is 254 °C·day (457.2 °F·day). Stage tables by variety go in
  `stages_by_variety`.
- **Alternate methods:**
  - **`jan1_4_4c`:** January 1 at 4.4 °C / 40 °F.
  - **`after_chill`:** the count starts the day the variety's chill requirement is met. Chill hours between 32 and
    45 °F are counted from November 1, estimated from each day's high and low. The requirement is per variety
    (`chill_hours_by_variety`), with 1,000 h as the default to calibrate.
- **Choosing a method:** a block's method is `by_block`, else its variety's `by_variety`, else `default_method`.
  `get_degree_days` (and Go/Hold, IPM, template scheduling) returns `method` with each answer: name, biofix rule,
  base, citation, what chose it, and the chill count where used.
- **Validator:** refuses an unknown method, a chill method without hours, a °C/°F mismatch, and any cherry method at
  45 °F or above. Pest models keep their own published bases (western cherry fruit fly stays 41 °F).

**On a site with crop_phenology@1 already published**

Draft a new version from the seed (`config action=draft kind=crop_phenology`), preview, and publish in the Desk.

**Build / server**

Push and build with `ERPNEXT_MCP_VERSION=0.310.0`, then `bench --site frontend migrate` and restart.

**Rollback**

Roll back to the v0.309.0 image. The methods are ignored and the row's own base applies.
