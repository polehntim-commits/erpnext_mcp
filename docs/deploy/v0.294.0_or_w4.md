**Deploy: v0.294.0 — Form OR-W-4 as its own form kind**

**Migrate.** W-4 Form gains `form_kind` (W-4 / OR-W-4), line 4a, the Redetermination box, "Oregon withholding based
on", the OR-W-4's own signature, date and IP, and the send-to-DOR fields. Tools stay at 1,142, and no new tool is
added. Some existing tools gain arguments:
- `submit_w4`: `form=or_w4`, `or_source`, `or_exemption_code`, `or_redetermination`, `or_expected_weekly_wages`;
- `list_employees_missing_w4`: `form=or_w4`;
- `render_w4_pdf`: `form=or_w4`, refused for now (see below).

The phone (app 0.59.0) is unchanged: it still files the OR-W-4 fields with the federal W-4, and that keeps working.

**3. Using it (HR)**

- **Record an OR-W-4:** `submit_w4 form=or_w4 employee=… company=… or_filing_status=… or_allowances=…`. Then collect
  the worker's signature with `collect_form_signature doctype="W-4 Form" field="or_signature"`. A new OR-W-4
  supersedes the old one; the federal W-4 is untouched.
- **No OR-W-4 on file:** use `or_source` to apply the fallback order in the 2026 instructions:
  1. "Pre-2020 Oregon-only W-4";
  2. "Pre-2020 federal W-4" (enter its marital status and allowances in `or_filing_status` / `or_allowances`);
  3. otherwise, 8%.

  "Refused" (the worker refused a certificate) withholds Single with 0 allowances (OAR 150-316-0290).
- **Who still needs one:** `list_employees_missing_w4 form=or_w4` lists who is on the flat 8% and which certificates
  must be sent to the DOR, with a send-by date. Set `or_forwarded_on` once a copy is sent.

**Review (CPA / HR)**

- **The send-to-DOR rule** (OAR 150-316-0290(4)): more than 10 allowances; exempt with expected wages over $200 a
  week; or a state-only exemption (an exempt claim with a line 4a code). "Within 20 days" is read as 20 days from the
  employee's signature. The thresholds are in payroll setting `oregon_withholding` → `forwarding`.
- **E-signature:** no Oregon DOR source was found on accepting electronic OR-W-4 signatures. Confirm with the DOR
  before relying on the e-signature alone.
- **Official PDF:** filling the official 150-101-402 PDF needs that form bundled with the app, the way the IRS W-4
  is. Downloading it needs Tim's OK; until then `render_w4_pdf form=or_w4` refuses rather than producing a lookalike.

Roll back to the v0.293.0 image. OR-W-4 rows filed on their own would then be read as federal W-4s, so before
rolling back, supersede them or re-enter the Oregon fields on the federal W-4.
