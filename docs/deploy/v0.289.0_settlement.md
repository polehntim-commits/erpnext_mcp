**3. Before turning daily pay on for a company (Tim): the parallel run**

1. Pick a past workweek (Sunday–Saturday) that has reviewed punches.
2. With `daily_pay_enabled` OFF: `preview_payroll_for_period` for the week (Regular). Note each worker's gross and
   net.
3. Turn `daily_pay_enabled` ON for the company. Mark one or two workers' `daily_pay` with `daily_pay_since` at or
   before that Sunday, and give them an active direct deposit.
4. For each day: `preview_payroll_for_period` with `run_kind=Daily` (start = end = the day). Then for the week:
   `run_kind=Settlement`. **Previews write nothing.**
5. Compare per worker. Gross should match except for minimum-wage makeup: it is per day under daily pay, so it can
   only be higher. Withholding differs by timing; the week's total is close. Every difference has a reason on the
   slips.
6. Turn the flag OFF again until the phone opt-in ships, or keep it on for the workers who signed.
