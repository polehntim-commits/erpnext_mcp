**What changed** (Tim, 2026-10-11: "go with the most accurate")
- **The 2026 figure:** RCW 6.27.150 exempts the greater of 85% or 50 × "the highest minimum wage law in the state".
  The 2026 Washington row now uses **Burien's $21.78**, its large-employer rate from the city's own page
  (https://burienwa.gov/city_hall/laws_regulations/minimum_wage; formula result $21.7752; council clarification
  2026-05-18).
  - It is the highest current official figure and the most protective, so an exemption based on it cannot
    under-protect.
  - The row records the source and a note: L&I's February 2026 table lists Burien at $21.63, which predates the
    council action; Tukwila is $21.65; litigation over Burien's ordinance is pending.
  - The weekly exemption floor is now $1,089.00 (it was $1,082.50 at $21.65).
- **A source is required:** the validator refuses a highest-local-minimum figure without its source.
- **Yearly review on the payroll calendar:**
  - **October 15:** cities publish next year's rates. It clears once a Washington row dated January 1 of next year
    is published.
  - **January 31:** check against L&I's January table. It clears once `lni_checked_on` is recorded that year; it is
    recorded as 2026-10-11 for 2026.
  - **2027 values are due by 2026-10-15** — the reminder shows as due now.
