**Deploy: v0.329.0 — "Can't take this": skippable checklist steps with a reason; one task screen driven by the template (with FarmOps 0.69.0 (65))**

**Migrate**: Farm Task Template Checklist Item gains `requirement`, `example_hint` and `follow_up_on_skip`. Tools:
**1,153 → 1,153** (no new tool; the template tools, `complete_farm_task`, `get_farm_task` and the phone's
`save_task_progress` / `get_task` carry more).

**Why** (Tim, after the 10/13 insurance-photo field test)
- "Some photos aren't hard requirements. If one can't be taken, the worker needs a way to log that."
- "Some of the tasks had specific photos it was looking for, others did not … usability not all that great yet."

**What changed**
- **Per checklist step, on the template:**
  - `requirement` is Required / Skippable with reason / Optional;
  - `example_hint` is shown on the phone;
  - `follow_up_on_skip` makes a skip raise a re-do task.
  - All three are settable through `create_farm_task_template` / `update_farm_task_template` (`checklist=[{item_name,
    evidence_type, requirement, example_hint, follow_up_on_skip}]`) and are snapshotted onto each new task.
    `required` stays in step with the requirement, so an older reader still treats a skippable step as one to do.
- **"Can't take this":**
  - a skip carries a reason (blocked / no access, unsafe, not on this asset, weather / light, equipment in the way,
    other), an optional note (required for "other") and an optional photo of what was in the way;
  - a skip meets the step only where the template makes it Skippable; a Required step refuses a skip and says how
    to change that;
  - it works offline: `save_task_progress kind=skip`, or `complete_farm_task checklist=[{item_name, skipped:true,
    skip_reason, skip_note}]`. A later tick clears the skip.
- **Recorded:**
  - each skip is on the task's checklist (`get_farm_task`, the Desk) and in the phone's progress (`met`, `skipped`,
    `skip_reason`);
  - the completion says it on the task timeline ("Could not be done: …") and returns `skipped_steps`;
  - a step with `follow_up_on_skip` raises one "Re-do: <step> — <task>" task (same place, the step Required),
    listed in `follow_up_tasks`.
- **Phone (0.69.0): one task screen from the template.**
  - The stepper shows one step per checklist item, each with its own labeled photo slot and the example hint, then
    Location and Findings.
  - "Can't take this" on a skippable step opens the reason picker, note and photo.
  - Complete is enabled when every required step is met.
  - It is the same on every task, whether raised from the phone, the Desk or MCP: the checklist no longer also
    shows as a second photo form on Complete.
