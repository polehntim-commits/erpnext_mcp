**Deploy: v0.309.0 — bonuses in the regular rate, overtime thresholds and the federal trap**

**No migrate.** These are new keys in the Pay Policy and pay-item policy, read with defaults. Tools stay at 1,142.

**What changed** (Tim, 2026-10-10: "make sure we are in compliance")
- **Washington:** non-discretionary and production bonuses are always in the overtime regular rate (L&I
  Administrative Policy ES.A.8.1 §§4.1, 5.3, 6.2). The validator refuses turning that off. Farm overtime is after 40
  hours since 2024-01-01; the source now cites RCW 49.46.130(6)(c).
- **Oregon bonuses — behaviour change:** bonuses are now IN the regular rate by default. This is the conservative
  choice and reverses v0.298.0's exclusion. OAR 839-020-0030(1)'s text leaves them out, so a published
  `pay_item_policy` may set `states.OR.bonuses_in_regular_rate=false` on an attorney's advice. The validator warns,
  and every run says so.
- **Oregon farm overtime:** 48 hours through 2026-12-31, 40 from 2027-01-01 (ORS 653.272, HB 4002), dated rows in the
  Wage Floor. The Pay Policy's `state_floor` mode pays exactly that; a farm threshold of 40 is a policy choice.
- **Piece-rate exemptions:** BOLI's three (500 or fewer piece-rate workdays a quarter, local hand-harvest commuters,
  immediate family; ORS 653.020(1)) are data flags, default OFF. Payroll never applies one, and the validator refuses
  `applied_by_payroll`.
- **The federal trap:** Pay Policy `flsa.handles_other_farms_crop`, default false. Handling another farm's crop loses
  the FLSA farm exemption (29 CFR 780.141-.143). When on, overtime is after 40 hours in every state, with bonuses in
  the regular rate, and every run warns.
- **Harvest-bonus disclosure** (OAR 839-020-0013): a bonus kind can carry a disclosure record (terms, posted_on,
  language, method) in `pay_item_policy.kinds`. It covers every bonus of that kind earned after it was posted.
  Runs still list bonuses with neither an item-level nor a kind-level disclosure.

**Run output**

`compliance_warnings` lists whichever of these flags are in effect.
