(`docs/deploy/v0.211.0_quick_wins.md`); deploying 0.212.0 delivers both.

## 1. Server

1. Push `erpnext_mcp` `main`; build the image at the commit that carries
   `__version__ = "0.212.0"`. No new Python packages.
2. `bench --site <site> migrate`. Expect:

     Training Session Attendee: `reminded_on`, and `Self` as a scan source;

3. The hourly scheduler now also runs `training_courses.send_reminders` (the evening-before
   push). It needs the APNs settings already used for task pushes; without them it reports
   and does nothing.
4. ERPNext MCP Settings, for §3: `allow_group_training_sessions`,
   `allow_add_training_session_day`.

## 2. App

## 3. The Applicator License renewal (Oct 28 and Nov 17), over MCP

```
list_training_sessions   training_type: "<the course>"   view: "all"
→ find the two sessions

group_training_sessions  sessions: ["<Oct 28 session>", "<Nov 17 session>"]
→ a course; the two become Day 1 and Day 2; everybody on either is registered on both
```

So a missed day names the licence it puts at risk, set on the Training Type in Desk:
**Renews Certification** = `Applicator License`.

A new multi-day class: `create_training_session` with
`days: [{session_date, start_time, end_time, location}, …]`.

On each day: scan badges (or attendees check in on their phones), sign, and
`complete_training_session` on **that day**. When the last required day is completed the
course completes itself and files one training record per person who attended every
required day. Anybody who missed one is listed and appears in the compliance inbox.

## 5. What to check after

- **Card:** the day before a class a registered attendee's Today shows "Class tomorrow";
  on the day, "Class today — Day N of M" with **Check in**.
- **Push:** at 18:00 site time the evening before, one notification per attendee.
- **Work:** a Foreman sees **Clock in crew** and **Assign tasks** tiles; a picker sees
  neither, and the server refuses a picker who calls the routes.
- **Assign:** on a task with a Required Certification, people without it are listed and
  cannot be chosen.
- **AFB-2026-00030** stays Open until Tim confirms on a device.

## 6. Decisions made here that Tim may want to reverse

- **Attendees can open their class's papers** (not the sign-in sheet or signatures). This
  was left for Tim in v0.211.0; the class card needs it. One gate
  (`mobile._is_class_attendee`) switches it back.
- **Self check-in counts as identification** (like a badge scan); the signature is still
  taken by whoever runs the class.
- **Assign is to one worker.** A task has one holder and nothing splits it into copies.
