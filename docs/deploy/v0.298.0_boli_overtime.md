**Deploy: v0.298.0 — farm overtime as BOLI computes it**

Tim, 2026-10-10: "align it all with BOLI"; overtime at the legal floor; bonuses as BOLI says. This is the first of
the BOLI-alignment releases.

**Migrate.** No new fields. The migrate:
- adds Pay Policy mode `state_floor`;
- adds a `states` rule to `pay_item_policy` (Oregon: bonuses are out of the regular rate);
- on a site whose published Pay Policy still uses the farm's own 40 hours, drafts **Pay Policy v2** in
  `state_floor` mode, effective the next Sunday.

Tools stay at 1,142.

**What changed** (sources: ORS 653.271–.273; OAR 839-020-0030; BOLI's agricultural minimum wage and overtime page)
1. **Threshold, per workweek and per state, dated** (ORS 653.272):
   - Oregon is **48 hours in 2025–26** and **40 from 2027-01-01**; Washington is 40.
   - A week worked in both states takes the lower threshold.
   - Each week on the run shows its threshold (`regular_rate_by_week`).
   - This takes effect when Pay Policy v2 is published. Until then the published policy's 40 hours still applies.
2. **Each workweek stands alone** (OAR 839-020-0030(2)(a)): the overtime premium is on that week's own regular
   rate, not the pay period's blend. Example: in a two-week period of $15/h and $22.50/h piece weeks, overtime in
   week one is paid at $15 × 0.5.
3. **The regular rate is never below minimum wage** (0030(2)(b)): a slow piece week's overtime premium is on the
   minimum wage. Gross still meets the same floor; the premium part is larger and the makeup smaller.
4. **The workweek is the employer's fixed one** (ORS 653.271(4), Pay Policy `workweek_start` = Sunday), not the
   pay period's first day. A period that starts mid-workweek is flagged (`workweek_warning`). Keep pay periods
   aligned to the Sunday workweek.
5. **Bonuses** (OAR 839-020-0030(1), "without benefits of … bonuses … profit sharing"):
   - For Oregon farm workers, bonuses and profit shares no longer add overtime. The bonus itself is still paid in
     full.
   - Washington keeps the v0.296.0 treatment. That treatment is unverified for Washington farm overtime; the CPA
     should check it.
6. **Comments and fallbacks** that called Oregon "40, fully phased" now cite ORS 653.272. The engine's last-resort
   minimum-wage table is labelled as superseded; every run uses the dated BOLI Wage Floor.

**1. Build (Tim)**

Push erpnext_mcp main (`__version__ = "0.298.0"`), then build with `ERPNEXT_MCP_VERSION=0.298.0`.

**2. Server**

```zsh
sudo docker exec -u frappe -w /home/frappe/frappe-bench fafo-erpnext_server_1 bench --site frontend migrate
sudo docker restart fafo-erpnext_server_1
```

Expect `payroll setting payroll_setting:pay_policy@2 drafted — publish it in the Desk …`.

**3. Tim, in the Desk**

Open Farm Config Version `payroll_setting:pay_policy@2` and check it:
- `overtime.mode` should be `state_floor`;
- `effective_from` should be the next Sunday.

Then run `preview_config kind=pay_policy` against last period, and **Publish**. From that date, Oregon farm
overtime starts after 48 hours a week (40 from 2027).

**Rollback**

Roll back to the v0.297.0 image. The overtime premium then goes back to the period average, and a published v2
stays harmless.
