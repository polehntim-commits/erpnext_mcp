**Deploy: v0.311.0 — garnishment: every limit, the most protective taken**

**Migrate**: Farm Payroll Deduction gains `debt_type`. Existing rows are not rewritten; a run derives their type from
the category and lists them under `debt_type_derived`. Tools stay at 1,142.

**What changed**
- **Debt type on every garnishment:** child support, spousal support, tax levy, federal student loan (AWG), private
  student loan, consumer debt, or other judgment.
  - `create_payroll_deduction` fills it in where the category settles it (support, tax levy) and refuses a
    garnishment where it doesn't (student loan, creditor) until it's named.
  - `create_garnishment` takes `debt_type`. A creditor order without one is taken as consumer debt, the most
    protective reading.
- **Every applicable limit is computed, and the lowest cap wins.** Each line carries `limits_considered`:
  - **Federal** (15 U.S.C. 1673): 25% or 30 × $7.25 ($217.50 a week); support 50/60%, plus 5% when over 12 weeks in
    arrears; federal student loans 15%.
  - **Oregon** (ORS 18.385, SB 1595): 75% exempt or the floor — $400 a week, $832 biweekly, $912 semimonthly, $1,792
    monthly from 2026-07-01, as dated rows (a new row each July 1). Support writs keep the $254-a-week floor.
    Support is deducted first.
  - **Washington** (RCW 6.27.150): the greater of 35 × the federal minimum ($253.75 a week) or 75%. Consumer debt:
    the greater of 35 × the WA minimum ($599.55) or 80%. Private student loans: the greater of 50 × the highest local
    minimum wage or 85%. Spousal maintenance: 50%.
