**Deploy: v0.306.0 — sick time, deduction authorizations and separations on the phone** (pairs with FarmOps 0.62.0 (58))

**No migrate.** No new fields. Tools stay at 1,142; one mobile route is added (`record_separation`).

**What changed**
- **Sick time is a leave type:** `list_leave_types` lists Oregon Sick Time and Washington Paid Sick Leave in hours.
  Each row carries the balance, what was accrued and used this year, and when it becomes usable.
  - For a worker, it shows the states they have sick time in or work in.
  - A site without the hrms app still lists them.
- **Filing from the phone:** the phone's `create_leave_request` passes `hours`, so a worker files sick hours from
  their own phone (My Records → Sick time).
- **Deduction authorizations:** `list_employee_deductions awaiting_authorization=true` lists the voluntary deductions
  a run would skip for want of the worker's signed authorization (ORS 652.610(3)). Every deduction row now says
  what it lacks (`authorization_missing`).
  - The phone opens the signature pad on each one (Employee → Pay and separation): review, badge, ink, evidence.
  - The pad posts through `submit_form_signature` to `Farm Payroll Deduction.authorization_signature`.
- **Separations:** `record_separation` (HR roles, Farm Manager included, as `update_employee`) records the last day, the reason and whether the worker is a seasonal
  farmworker. It answers when final pay is due (ORS 652.140 / 652.145).
  - It sets the worker to Left once the last day has passed.
  - Marking final pay paid stays in the Desk.

**Switches**

Filing sick time needs `allow_create_leave_request` and `allow_list_leave_types`. A separation needs
`allow_update_employee`. All of these are already on wherever leave and employee edits are used.

**Build / server**

Push and build with `ERPNEXT_MCP_VERSION=0.306.0`, then `bench --site frontend migrate` (nothing new) and restart.
Install FarmOps 0.62.0 (58).

**Rollback**

Roll back to the v0.305.0 image. The new phone screens then get "not found" on `record_separation`, and the sick
time rows disappear from the leave picker.
