**Deploy: v0.321.0 — which lens took the photo (with FarmOps 0.64.0 (60))**

**Migrate** (Farm Task Evidence gains two read-only columns). Tools: **1,150 → 1,150**.

**Why**

Tim: photo capture must zoom out, so he doesn't have to walk back to fit a cabin, building or block in the frame.
FarmOps 0.64.0 opens the ultra-wide lens where the phone has one (0.5× and up, pinch to zoom, a lens selector,
the last choice remembered per kind of photo). This release keeps the lens on the record.

**What changed**
- **Farm Task Evidence** gains `capture_lens` (ultra_wide / wide / telephoto / front) and `zoom_factor` (0.5 =
  ultra-wide, 1 = main lens). Both are read-only and filled from the phone.
- **The phone sends them per photo** beside `captured_at` and the GPS fix. An unknown lens or an unreadable zoom
  is dropped, never refused; the photo always files.
- **Every evidence JPEG from 0.64.0 also carries them in its own EXIF:**
  - DateTimeOriginal;
  - GPS where the phone had a fix;
  - LensModel "FarmOps ultra_wide 0.5x";
  - a UserComment.

  That covers photos with no column for them: inspection photos, asset and structure photos, safety reports,
  receipts and onboarding documents.
- **No change to receipt OCR:** document cameras open at 1× every time.

**After deploy**

1. Complete a task on 0.64.0 with a 0.5× photo. The assignment's Evidence row shows Capture Lens `ultra_wide`
   and Zoom Factor 0.5.
2. `run_post_deploy_check expected_version=0.321.0`.

**Build / server**

Push and build with `ERPNEXT_MCP_VERSION=0.321.0`, then `bench --site frontend migrate` and restart.

**Phone**

FarmOps **0.64.0 (60)** to TestFlight. An older server ignores the two keys, so the app can ship before or after
this release.

**Rollback**

Roll back to the v0.320.0 image. The two columns stay, empty; the phone's extra keys are ignored.
