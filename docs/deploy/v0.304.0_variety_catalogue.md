**Deploy: v0.304.0 — the variety catalogue per crop**

**Migrate**: Crop Variety gains `aliases` (Small Text) and `is_pollinizer` (Check). Nothing is seeded or rewritten:
existing rows get blank aliases and are not pollinizers. Tools stay at 1,142.

**What changed**
- **Aliases:** a Crop's Varieties table (its catalogue) takes other spellings per variety ("Bing Cherry"). A field
  naming an alias, in its `variety` column or its Varieties table, is written back as the catalogue spelling, and
  `get_variety_care_recipe` finds a variety by alias.
- **Refused:** an alias that is another variety's own name, or one that two varieties share.
- **Pollinizer flag:** per variety (Van for Bing).
- **Fixed:** `update_crop varieties=[…]` used to drop `target_brix`; it now keeps it. Varieties also take `aliases`
  and `is_pollinizer`.
- **Starting a catalogue:** `update_crop seed_from_fields=true` appends a row for each variety the crop's fields
  name that the catalogue doesn't know by name or alias. It never merges two spellings; an alias does that, and a
  person decides it. Same switch as `update_crop` (default OFF).

**Watch for**

Once a crop has a catalogue, a field naming a variety outside it is refused (since v0.277.0). Seed from the fields
first, then add aliases for the variant spellings before trimming rows.

**Build / server**

Push and build with `ERPNEXT_MCP_VERSION=0.304.0`, then `bench --site frontend migrate` and restart.

**Rollback**

Roll back to the v0.303.0 image. The two new columns stay in the table and are ignored. A field already written
back to the catalogue spelling keeps it.
