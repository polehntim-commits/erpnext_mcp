# v0.124.0 — the seventy-third route, and the test that would not have noticed

One mobile route, and a repair to the assertion that was supposed to make the
last seventy-two impossible to get wrong.

## The audit that prompted it

v0.123.0 mounted seventy-two methods and its notes claimed the HACCP,
traceability and food-safety surface was closed. Re-derived from the registry
rather than from the release notes, that holds: all twenty-eight methods named
in the brief — the food safety plans, hazard analyses, preventive controls,
monitoring and corrective-action records, recall plans and drills, supplier and
verification records, the lot register, `record_cte`, both directions of the
trace and `index_lot_events` — have a wrapper and a route. So do the sensor,
MRL, soil-compaction, variety-care and competitive registers.

Sixty-five MCP tools were added between v0.116.0 and v0.123.0. Fifty-six of them
are mounted. Nine are not, and they fall into two groups.

## `list_tasks_by_location`

Shipped as a tool in v0.122.0 with the HACCP work and missed by v0.123.0's
routing pass, which swept the new registers and not `fieldwork`. It groups the
tasks a worker holds together with the pool they may take from, by place, so a
screen shows one trip's worth of work instead of two lists to cross-reference.

It is open on enrolment, and it could not honestly be anything else: it is a
third reader of `list_my_tasks` and `list_available_for_me`, both already
mounted open, and it answers for the authenticated caller alone. A gate on it
would close a screen onto the caller's own work while leaving the two calls it
is assembled from wide open — refusing nothing, and costing a trip.

`worker_id` is not accepted, for the reason `list_my_tasks` does not accept
`employee`.

## The eight that were deliberately left off

`create`/`get`/`list`/`update_strategic_plan` and the four matching
`strategic_objective` methods, from v0.118.0. These are not field methods. A
Strategic Plan holds the vision, the SWOT, Porter's five forces and the exit;
the writes supersede a farm's stated strategy. `routes.py` has always kept a
class of tool unreachable from a handset at any path rather than gating it —
`create_journal_entry`, `convey_parcel` and `import_chart_of_accounts` are named
in its own docstring — and if the competitive register was worth an HR gate
because what this farm thinks a rival is worth is not a picker's, the exit
strategy is not a picker's either. Mounting them is a decision the operation
should take deliberately, not one that arrives under "and the rest".

## The assertion that was not doing its job

`TheNewRegistersAreGated.test_the_three_sets_are_exactly_the_routes_this_release_added`
said in its own docstring that "a route added later and left out of this file
fails here rather than going quietly ungated". It did not. It asserted that
every method NAMED in the three gate sets is mounted — the direction that cannot
go wrong by itself — and never that every mounted route is named. The failure
mode it existed to catch was the other one.

This was proved rather than reasoned about: `list_tasks_by_location` was mounted
with no entry in any of the three sets, and all four tests stayed green.

The count of mounted `/mobile` routes is now checked against the surface as it
stood at v0.122.0, so a route added after that release must be classified as
dispatch-gated, HR-gated or open-on-enrolment, or the test fails. Restoring the
old omission now fails with the sentence that explains it. The route table goes
from 274 to 275; `ROUTES` also carries the two `/files` paths, which is why the
check counts `/mobile` alone.
